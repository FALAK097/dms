## Design Context

### Users
Internal teams and clients managing shared document libraries. They upload and browse files, connect supported storage, and ask questions grounded in source documents.

### Brand Personality
Clear, considered, dependable.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use DMS's existing document logo and brand. Take Devin's reference as a cue for spacious composition, strong left-aligned type, quiet neutral surfaces, and an authentic product preview. Keep visuals and copy specific to DMS.

### Design Principles
- Let the actual document workspace be the main visual.
- Use concise, accurate copy with one clear primary action.
- Keep color and motion restrained, with readable contrast in both themes.
- Preserve DMS logo, existing auth flow, responsive behavior, and integrations.
